# Basal Cognition

Which minimal systems sense, remember, learn and regulate behavior, and how can those capacities be tested? Covers simple neural and non-neural organisms, and controls that distinguish learning from adaptation, fatigue or selection.

## Capacities and behavioral organization

- **Plant cognition criteria** - Adams requires cognitive representations beyond information processing; Segundo-Ortin and Calvo defend flexible, anticipatory plant behavior as cognitive. The dispute concerns both mechanisms and what counts as cognition. [Adams 2018](<https://doi.org/10.1016/j.shpsa.2017.11.007> "Cognition wars; Stud. Hist. Philos. Sci."), [Segundo-Ortin and Calvo 2019](<https://miguelsegundoortinphd.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/segundo-ortincalvo_2019_areplantscognitive_publishedversion.pdf> "Are plants cognitive? A reply to Adams; Stud. Hist. Philos. Sci.").
- **Stentor avoidance hierarchy** - Repeated stimulation elicits an ordered repertoire of bending, ciliary changes, contraction and detachment in Stentor roeseli, despite individual variation. Replicates Jennings with the correct species; the hierarchy differs from habituation. [Dexter et al. 2019](<https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2019.10.059> "A Complex Hierarchy of Avoidance Behaviors in a Single-Cell Eukaryote; Curr. Biol.").
- **Basal cognitive capacities** - Organizes cognition around sensing, memory, learning and regulation across simple organisms with and without nervous systems. Proposes a research framework grounded in biological functions, rather than starting from human mental categories. [Lyon et al. 2021](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7935032/> "Reframing cognition: getting down to biological basics; Phil. Trans. R. Soc. B").
- **Operational agency** - The Technological Approach to Mind Everywhere compares interventions for predicting and controlling systems, from rewiring to training. Its authors distinguish goal pursuit from consciousness and affirm compatibility with materialism. [Levin 2022](<https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/systems-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnsys.2022.768201/full> "Technological Approach to Mind Everywhere: An Experimentally-Grounded Framework for Understanding Diverse Bodies and Minds; Front. Syst. Neurosci."), [Levin and Resnik 2026](<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13752-025-00524-5> "Mind Everywhere: A Framework for Conceptualizing Goal-Directedness in Biology and Other Domains—Part Two; Biological Theory").

## Learning and alternative explanations

- **Physarum habituation** - Repeated exposure reduces avoidance of quinine or caffeine barriers. Response recovery after withdrawal and stimulus specificity support habituation in this slime-mould assay. [Boisseau et al. 2016](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4855389/> "Habituation in non-neural organisms: evidence from slime moulds; Proc. R. Soc. B").
- **Evidence for single-cell learning** - Reassesses historical learning experiments and reasons for their dismissal. Argues that associative learning merits renewed investigation with stronger controls; a review of disputed evidence, not a new replication. [Gershman et al. 2021](<https://elifesciences.org/articles/61907> "Reconsidering the evidence for learning in single cells; eLife").
- **Cellular spacing effect** - Four spaced chemical pulses induce more persistent gene-reporter expression than a duration-matched pulse in two human cell lines. Inhibiting signaling and transcription pathways disrupts this molecular spacing effect. [Kukushkin et al. 2024](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11544106/> "The massed-spaced learning effect in non-neural human cells; Nat. Commun.").

## Origins of capacities

- **Capacities without direct selection** - E. coli selected in one environment gains traits useful elsewhere. Levin proposes that embodiments access pre-existing patterns and minds; latent capacities alone do not distinguish this ontology from evolutionary mechanisms. [Karve and Wagner 2022](<https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-33634-w> "Environmental complexity is more important than mutation in driving the evolution of latent novel traits in E. coli; Nat. Commun."), [Levin 2026](<https://doi.org/10.3390/philosophies11050161> "Ingressing Minds: Causal, Non-Physical Patterns In-Form Natural, Synthetic, and Hybrid Embodiments; Philosophies").
- **Pattern-guided exploration** - In simulated mazes, combining fractal and sensory inputs improves trained agents' coverage over sensory-only inputs. Replacing fractals with uniform noise yields no significant change, leaving a fractal-specific benefit unresolved. [Ertle et al. 2025](<https://hrilab.tufts.edu/publications/ertleetal25icdl.pdf> "Free Lunch? Low-Cost Intelligence Through Pattern-Guided Exploration; ICDL").

## Open questions

- Does a changed population response reflect changes within tracked cells, differences in survival and reproduction, or both?
- Which capacities transfer across tasks and preparations, and which depend on a particular assay?
- Which predictions distinguish a proposed source of novel capacities from inherited components, physical dynamics and external training?
